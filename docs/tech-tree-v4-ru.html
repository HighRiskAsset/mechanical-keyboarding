Draft 2026-09-11, fourth cut. Materials and machines. Some materials are raw. A recipe at a machine turns input materials into one output material, and every recipe has its own. A lesson is one recipe or one raw. Prices are made of materials. Built by dev/tech-tree-v4-build.js from the lesson plan and docs/lessons-v4-ru.mech.js. Ids only.

Counts

  • 13 raws, one mine each (I-01, I-02, I-03, I-05, I-07, I-09, I-11, I-13, I-15, I-17, I-19, I-21, I-23).
  • 111 materials in all: the 13 raws and 98 made ones, one per recipe. Automation is the only thing that retires a lesson.
  • 84 recipes at 12 machines, at most 11 each. A machine has a shape (belts and pipes in, belts and pipes out); a recipe goes to an open machine of its shape, and a machine of a new shape opens when a recipe needs one: M1 2 belts in → 1 belt out at C2 · M2 1 belt in → 2 belts out at C4 · M3 3 belts in → 1 belt out at C4 · M4 1 belt in → 1 belt out at C6 · M5 2 belts in → 2 belts out at C6 · M6 1 belt + 1 pipe in → 1 belt + 1 pipe out at C7 · M7 2 belts in → 3 belts out at C9 · M8 2 belts in → 1 belt out at C13 · M9 2 belts + 1 pipe in → 1 belt + 1 pipe out at C15 · M10 3 belts in → 1 belt out at C17 · M11 2 belts in → 1 belt out at C23 · M12 2 belts in → 1 belt out at pages 5. Machines do not gate lessons; a recipe's inputs do.
  • 5 fluids travel by pipe, 14 byproducts come out beside their lesson's material (one from 8 lessons, two from 3); the pages are printed on them and machines are built with them, in turn.
  • 97 lessons = 13 raws + 84 recipes. 129 purchases.
