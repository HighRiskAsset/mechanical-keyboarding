Sky proof sheet

Grades one slice of a real world through the real SKY layer, once per hour of the day and once per sky, and POSTs the contact sheet to assets/inbox/ through the dev server's /upload. The ground under it is the same bake dev/map.html shows, with two machine bodies stood on it so the light pools have something to fall on. Every cell is a real frame out of the renderer, not a mock-up. Not linked from the game.
